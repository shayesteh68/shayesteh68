# Narges Shayesteh

**Bioinformatics Analyst · MSc Biophysics, IASBS · RNA-seq & single-cell transcriptomics**

I work with R/Bioconductor and Python on transcriptomics projects using public or client-authorized data. My independent portfolio demonstrates count-matrix and sample-metadata checks, DESeq2 analysis, Seurat workflows, pseudobulk analysis, marker review, and reproducible reporting.

I take on small, clearly scoped research-data QA, workflow review, and transcriptomics tasks. Before work starts, I agree on the input, study design, deliverables, data handling, and timeline. I report assumptions and limitations; exploratory analyses are not clinical validation or independent experimental confirmation.

## Selected projects

- [LOY bladder-cancer RNA-seq](https://github.com/shayesteh68/LOY-BladderCancer-ImmuneEvasion-GSE290114) — independent DESeq2 reanalysis of six MB49 samples; [source study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09071-2).
- [Paired ccRCC RNA-seq](https://github.com/shayesteh68/ccRCC_GSE278174_salami) — paired-patient reanalysis with a provenance-checked sample map; [source study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-35021-7).
- [Harmony integration example](https://github.com/shayesteh68/scRNAseq_Harmony_Integration) — exploratory Seurat/Harmony integration of the Kang IFN-beta PBMC dataset; [source study](https://doi.org/10.1038/nbt.4042). Differential expression uses raw-count donor-by-condition pseudobulk.
- [Barrington snRNA-seq](https://github.com/shayesteh68/barrington-snrna-sex-dimorphism) — independent reanalysis of the [source atlas](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-45907-7), with pool-level pseudobulk and provisional annotations; also compares scope with a [related Bar-Penk study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-77949-4).
- [Drosophila Stage 6 scRNA-seq](https://github.com/shayesteh68/Drosophila_scRNA_Stage6) — independent Seurat v5 reanalysis of [Karaiskos et al.’s dataset](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aan3235); the Src42A target-gene focus is informed by a [related study from Arno Müller’s group](https://doi.org/10.1242/dev.201119).
- [PBMC3K Seurat workflow](https://github.com/shayesteh68/scrnaseq-seurat-pbmc3k) — independent single-donor workflow example using the [10x PBMC3K dataset](https://cf.10xgenomics.com/samples/cell-exp/1.1.0/pbmc3k/pbmc3k_web_summary.html), with exploratory clusters and provisional cell labels.
